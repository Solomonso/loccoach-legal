Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TrainWith ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the TrainWith iOS app, where members of a gym post planned training sessions and join each other's.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and sign-in method (email and password, or Sign in with Apple). If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we receive Apple's private relay address instead.
  • Profile: your full name, a profile photo, your gym, the workout types you do and your experience level. Other members never see your full name: they see your first name and last initial (for example "Sofia K."), your photo, your workout types and your experience level.
  • Sessions: the sessions you post (day, start time, workout type and an optional note) and the sessions you join. Members of your gym can see them until a few hours after the session starts.
  • Safety: the members you block, and the reports you make about other members.
  • Notifications: a push notification token for your device, if you allow notifications, and whether you want session alerts.
  • Usage analytics: which screens and features you use, an app instance identifier, your device type and an approximate location (country or city level) derived from your IP address. We link this to an internal account identifier, never to your name or email.
  • Assistant (optional): if you turn on the Assistant, the words you type to it, and how many requests you made today. See The Assistant below.

We do not collect your device's advertising identifier and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use your information

We use your information to run TrainWith: to set up your account and profile, show your sessions to members of your gym and theirs to you, let members join each other's sessions, notify you about new sessions, joins and cancellations you've opted into, keep members safe through blocking and reporting, and understand which features are used so we can improve the app. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

The Assistant

The Assistant fills in a session for you from what you type, such as "legs tomorrow at six". It is off until you agree on the screen that explains it, and you can turn it off again at any time in Profile.

  • What is sent: the words you type, today's date and the list of session start times. These go to Anthropic, our AI provider, which returns the fields of a draft session for you to check.
  • What is never sent: your name, photo, email, profile, or anything about other members.
  • What is kept: we do not save your conversations with the Assistant. We keep only a count of how many requests you made today, to enforce a daily limit, and delete it the next day. Anthropic deletes what it receives within 30 days, unless its automated safety systems flag it, in which case it may keep it for up to 2 years. Anthropic does not use it to train its models.
  • Nothing is posted for you: a draft becomes a session only when you tap Post.

Who we share information with

We use the following service providers to run TrainWith. Each processes only what's needed to provide their service, on our behalf:

  • Supabase — our database, file storage and sign-in provider. Your account, profile, photo, sessions and safety data are stored here, in the EU (Ireland).
  • Google Firebase — delivers push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and provides usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase).
  • Anthropic — processes what you type to the Assistant, only if you turn it on.
  • Apple — authenticates you if you choose Sign in with Apple, and delivers push notifications to your iPhone.

Google and Anthropic may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard under the GDPR.

Legal bases

We process your account, profile, session and notification-token data to provide the service you signed up for (performance of a contract). We send push notifications and use the Assistant only with your consent, which you can withdraw in the app at any time. We process reports and blocks, and usage analytics, on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping members safe and improving the app.

Data retention and deletion

Sessions disappear from the app a few hours after they start. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your profile, photo, sessions, joins, blocks, device tokens and sign-in account, and asks Google to delete the analytics data linked to your account. Reports you made or that were made about you are kept so they can still be followed up, but are no longer linked to your account.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile at any time in the app, turn notifications and the Assistant on or off in Profile, and delete your account in Profile → Delete account. Under the GDPR you also have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to restrict or object to how we process it, and to withdraw consent. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or the supervisory authority where you live.

Children's privacy

TrainWith is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.
